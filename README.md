# Stranded #
During an exo-ecological survey, your ship suffered a fuel leak while landing on the target planet, leaving you stranded.
The crew has now gone missing, and as the recorder, you must track down their whereabouts while searching for usable energy sources.
The planet’s ecosystem is highly unusual—remember to consult the biological logs left behind by the crew; they will help you survive the ordeal.

A first-person adventure puzzle game.
## Key Instructions ##
WASD -- Move

Space -- Jump

LeftMouseButton -- Use Item/Shoot

T -- Tablet

F -- Interact

M -- Select Teleport Point (For testing and debugging)

## Known Issues ##
There is currently no exit button in the game. Please use Alt+F4 to quit.

There are currently no invisible walls in the game. You may fall outside the map. If this happens, please press M to teleport back.

After being shot down a second time, the Sky Manta will not respawn, and you will have to restart the game.
